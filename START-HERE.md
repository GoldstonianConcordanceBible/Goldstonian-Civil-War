# START HERE — The Goldstonian Civil War

## What This Project Is

The Goldstonian Civil War is a living historical-media, educational, theological, memetics, and provenance research corpus.

It documents Civil War fieldwork, family history, film production, historical interpretation, social media, artificial intelligence, cultural narratives, and experimental onchain research.

The project is designed so that the original evidence, later interpretation, and eventual outcomes remain distinguishable.
